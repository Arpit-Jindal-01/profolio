## RUSH WORK

- **+25–50% of base price** depending on timeline urgency.
- Rush applies when turnaround is needed faster than standard delivery time.
- Standard delivery timelines are agreed at project start.

---

## PAYMENT TERMS

### Direct Client Payment (Preferred)

- **50% upfront** before work begins  
- **50% before final delivery** (upon approval of final draft)

### Small Projects (under ₹1,500)

- **100% upfront** is acceptable and preferred.

### Invoicing

- Payment via UPI, bank transfer, or platform escrow (Fiverr/Upwork where applicable).
- Work does not begin until upfront payment is confirmed.

## SOURCE FILE POLICY

- Source files (`.psd`, `.ai`, `.fig`, `.xlsx`, `.prproj`, `.aep`) are **not automatically included** in final delivery.
- Source files may be provided for an **additional fee** — discussed at project start.
- Final deliverables are provided in client-ready export formats (PDF, PNG, MP4, XLSX, etc.).

---

## SCOPE CHANGES

- Additional work **outside the agreed scope** is quoted separately before execution.
- Do not automatically start out-of-scope work without a new agreement.
- Scope-creep additions should be documented and billed accordingly.

## MULTI-SERVICE PACKAGES

- Target package advantage: **approximately 5–15%** when combining multiple services.
- Do not automatically discount multi-service projects — package pricing depends on scope, timeline and complexity.
- All packages must be explicitly agreed upon before work begins.
